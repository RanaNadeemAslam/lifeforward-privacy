Life Forward — Privacy Policy

App: Life Forward — Life After Foster Care (com.lifeforward.transition.toolkit)
Developer: T.L. Butler · Contact: ncmhcepreptutor@gmail.com
Effective date: October 27, 2026

Summary. Life Forward has no accounts and never sells your data. Everything you enter is encrypted and stored only on your phone, and it is never backed up to the cloud. Nothing you enter is ever sent to us or stored on a server. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google's ad and purchase services collect, described below.

1. What stays on your phone

Vital Vault documents and photos; journal entries, highlights and sticky notes; your job profile and application autofill answers (legal and preferred name, physical and mailing addresses, phone, email, work eligibility, work history, internships, volunteer history, education, skills and certifications, up to 5 references, military service and availability, plus — only if you choose to enter them — criminal record details and voluntary EEO survey answers); your application log, résumés and cover letters; your budget, bills (with how often each is due and an optional link to your biller's own https website), payday and calendar events; cleaning and hygiene checklists; lesson progress, badges, your daily streak and the savings amounts you log and your savings goals; an optional first name; and your PIN, stored only as a one-way hash. Life Forward never asks for or stores card, bank or Social Security numbers. We, the developer, cannot see, access or recover any of it.

2. Ads and purchases

On the free plan, Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads. When required, you can change your choices in Me › Privacy & settings › Ad privacy choices. Google Play Billing handles the $5/month ad-free subscription; we never receive payment details. Subscribers see no ads, and the ad service isn't loaded for them. On the free plan, an ad appears about every 5 minutes at a natural break; you can close it when the ad allows. When your subscription ends, the app asks whether to renew or continue free with ads.

3. Reminders, calls and links

Application follow-ups arrive seven calendar days after applying at 9 AM local, only while Applied. Deleting an application or advancing its status removes its follow-up. Interview reminders arrive 2 hours before the interview date and time you enter, only while the status is Interview; changing the status or deleting the application removes it. Tapping a reminder opens the matching screen (Money › Bills, Work › Applications or Checklists) only after the app is unlocked with your PIN or fingerprint. The daily checklist reminder is off until you turn it on in Tools › Checklists (7 PM unless you pick another time); it repeats each day, its setting is stored encrypted, and My data › Progress & wins or Delete all turns it off. Me › Privacy & settings shows whether your phone allows reminders; Turn on reminders asks Android again, or opens the phone's notification settings for Life Forward if Android won't ask. Nothing is sent anywhere. All notification text is generic: no employer, job title, bill name or amount. Reminder IDs are stable, separated by kind and stored encrypted; Delete all cancels notifications and erases their IDs.

If a call or message cannot be opened, the app shows the number and lets you copy it. This also works while the app is locked, without opening your saved information.

Reminders use your device timezone, with UTC as a fallback if it cannot be read. This does not access your location or send anything off your phone.

Bill, payday and checklist reminders are created on your phone. On the lock screen they show only that a reminder arrived — never bill names or amounts. Hotline buttons (988, Crisis Text Line, The Trevor Project, the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 911) open your phone's dialer or messages. Links to government, career and other websites open in your browser and follow those sites' own privacy policies.

4. How your information is protected

If camera access or opening a Vault photo fails, the app shows a message without deleting your existing document.

Vault photos are encrypted as soon as you take or choose them. The temporary copy the camera or photo picker leaves in the app's cache is then overwritten and deleted, and Delete all and every app start clear any copy left behind.

Fingerprint or face unlock is offered only on supported devices with biometrics enrolled. If it cannot complete, your app PIN is still required.

If saved information cannot be opened, that section starts empty and the app shows a notice. Its encrypted file is kept aside on this phone, still excluded from backups, until you use Delete all. Other saved sections remain available.

AES-256 encryption with a key kept in the Android Keystore. A 4 to 8 digit PIN you create (easy PINs like 1111 or 1234 aren't allowed), plus fingerprint or face unlock; the phone's own pattern can't stand in for your PIN. Wrong PINs trigger growing lockouts. The app locks again after a time you choose (immediately, 30 seconds, 1 or 5 minutes). Screenshots and screen recording are blocked, and the app is hidden in the recent-apps view. Copied job information is cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds, and the keyboard doesn't learn what you type in private fields. All connections use HTTPS. Downloads happen only when you tap Download, confirm and enter your PIN; you choose where Android saves the file, the app needs no storage permission, and file names never include your name. A downloaded copy is outside the app's encryption and PIN, so keep it private and delete it when you're done. No method is perfectly secure, so keep your phone's own screen lock on too.

5. Deleting your information

A wrong PIN shows a message and nothing is deleted. Delete all also clears your optional first name and every reminder. You can remove just your first name in Me or My data.

Delete any single item — an application, résumé, cover letter, journal entry, sticky note, bill, savings entry or event — at any time; the app asks first. Delete a whole section in Privacy & Security › My data. Or tap Delete all my data: the app asks “Delete all your information?” and nothing is erased until you tap “Yes, delete everything” (and enter your PIN, if you set one). Deleted information can't be recovered. Uninstalling the app also erases it.

6. What we don't do

No account, email or phone number required. No selling or renting of personal information. No analytics or tracking beyond the ad service described above. No access to your contacts or location. No cloud backups of your data.

7. Age

Life Forward is designed for adults 18 and older who are leaving foster care. It is not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Your rights

Because your information stays on your phone, you control it directly. For data collected by Google (ads and purchases), use your Google account settings or contact Google. Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Residents of some states and countries have additional rights; contact us and we'll help.
